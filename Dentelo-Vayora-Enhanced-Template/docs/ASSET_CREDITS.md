## Inherited images

The following provided assets are reused as illustrative template imagery: `hero-banner.png`, `about-banner.png`, `service-banner.png`, `service-icon-1.png` through `service-icon-6.png`, and `blog-1.jpg` through `blog-3.jpg`. They are not represented as photographs of a specific clinic or its team. The starter’s four sample doctor portrait files and two unused banner/background assets were removed from the refreshed package to avoid presenting stock portraits as real clinicians and to reduce unused weight. Add licensed, verified team portraits under `assets/images/` when publishing actual profiles.

No new third-party photos or image-generation assets were added to this customization.
